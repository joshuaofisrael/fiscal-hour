The S&P 500 gained 184.81 points, or 2.51%, in the week ended Friday 13 November to close at 7,561.67, [according to Dow Jones Market Data published by Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/2026111310378/sp-500-rises-251-this-week-to-756167-data-talk). It was the index's largest one-week gain since the week ending 7 August and snapped a four-week losing streak.

## A softer finish

The week ended on a weaker note. The index fell 30.73 points, or 0.40%, on Friday, its largest one-day decline since 5 November, and was down 0.76% over the final two sessions. It remains 3.29% below its record close of 7,818.93 on 6 October, and 12.29% higher than a year earlier.

## Energy and industrials led

The broad Morningstar US Market Index rose 2.52%, [Morningstar's weekly market wrap](https://www.morningstar.com/markets/market-wrap-stocks-up-252-led-by-energy-industrials) said. Energy was the best-performing sector, up 5.53%, followed by industrials, up 3.79%. Basic materials, up 0.49%, and real estate, up 0.7%, lagged but still rose. Mid-cap stocks gained 3.01%, ahead of large caps at 2.61% and small caps at 1.92%, and the Nasdaq rose 2.27%.

Among stocks covered by Morningstar analysts, The Trade Desk, AMD, Tandem Diabetes Care, Mobility Global and Broadcom posted the biggest gains, with The Trade Desk up 21.31%. Kanzhun, Ionis Pharmaceuticals, Hims & Hers Health, Corteva and IREN fell the most, Kanzhun down 26.55%.

## Yields kept rising

Stocks rallied even as bond yields rose. The 10-year Treasury yield climbed 0.294 percentage point over the week to 5.152%, [Morningstar reported](https://www.morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/202611139683/10-year-treasury-yield-rises-to-5152-this-week-data-talk), its longest run of daily increases since late September. The yield is 0.158 point below its 52-week high of 5.310% set on 5 October.
