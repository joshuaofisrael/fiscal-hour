Amazon may be about to write one of its biggest cheques in years. "Amazon closes in on $7.5bn deal to buy AI start-up Decart," [the Financial Times posted on X](https://x.com/FT/status/2124194480999813154) on Saturday. Relaying the report, [zerohedge posted](https://x.com/zerohedge/status/2124192019916497337) that the deal "could come together as soon as early next week."

Neither Amazon nor Decart has announced anything. The reports cite people familiar with the talks.

## What Amazon would be buying

According to [Livemint's summary of the FT report](https://www.livemint.com/companies/news/amazon-eyes-7-5-billion-decart-deal-to-boost-ai-chip-efficiency-challenge-nvidia-5-things-to-know-11795310250674.html), the talks are at an advanced stage. Decart makes software that lets AI models run more efficiently, freeing up chip capacity, which could help Amazon Web Services compete with Nvidia. Decart was also an early user of Amazon's own Trainium3 chip.

Israel's [Calcalist reported on 18 November](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/uckadwd7r) that Decart's software lets developers switch more easily between different AI chips, and that it also builds "world models" called Lucy and Oasis. Calcalist said Amazon would pay more than $7 billion in cash, stock and deferred payments, with part of the price tied to targets, taking the total to nearly $7.5 billion.

## From $4bn to $7.5bn in six months

Decart was founded in 2023. Livemint says it was valued at $4 billion in May after raising $300 million from investors including Sequoia Capital, Benchmark and Nvidia. A $7.5 billion price would be close to double that.

The road here was not smooth. Calcalist reports that Anthropic halted talks to buy Decart in early September after due diligence was effectively done, and that Nvidia, also an investor, declined to reopen its own talks. Calcalist says the three founders have agreed to stay for five years, with a significant part of the payment due only in year five.

## Why investors should care

Calcalist puts the deal among Amazon's largest in recent years, alongside the MGM studio purchase in 2022 and this year's agreed Globalstar deal. Next to Amazon's market value of about $2.67 trillion, cited by Livemint, the money is small.

The signal is bigger than the size. The bet is on squeezing more work out of chips Amazon already has, not only buying more. It comes as Livemint notes the Nasdaq 100 just had its worst week since the Covid crash, down 10.4%. If a deal is signed early this week, watch how AWS says it will use Decart's software with Trainium.
