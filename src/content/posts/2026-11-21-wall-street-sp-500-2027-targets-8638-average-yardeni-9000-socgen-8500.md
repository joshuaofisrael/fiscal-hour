The AI selloff has not shaken Wall Street's forecasters. "Wall Street remains broadly bullish despite the AI-driven selloff, with an eight-strategist survey averaging 8,638, implying roughly 20% upside," the [*Walter Bloomberg account posted on X](https://x.com/DeItaone/status/2123762498629226643) on Friday.

## The targets

The post lists these year-end 2027 targets for the S&P 500:

- Yardeni Research: 9,000
- Goldman Sachs: 8,700
- Morgan Stanley: 8,700
- Barclays: 8,700
- Société Générale: 8,500
- UBS Global Wealth Management: 8,200
- Wells Fargo: 8,200
- Ned Davis Research: 8,000

"Strategists expect double-digit earnings growth despite elevated interest rates and AI spending concerns," the post says. The figures come from that summary, so treat them as a snapshot: banks update their calls often.

## SocGen's case, in its own words

One target can be checked directly. [Reuters reported](https://x.com/Reuters/status/2123847634951520311) that Société Générale expects the index to reach 8,500 by the end of 2027, "driven by continued support from AI-related investments and resilient corporate earnings." According to the [Reuters story](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/socgen-sees-sp-500-ending-2027-8500-ai-earnings-support), that implies about 18.5% upside from Thursday's close of 7,175.83.

The French bank's path runs through the Federal Reserve. It expects two more quarter-point rate hikes, with the last one in March 2027, and says that should trigger a "re-rating phase", with the strongest returns in the second quarter. It kept its 2027 earnings growth forecast for the S&P 500 at 11% and told investors to "maintain high equity exposure". For Europe, it sees the STOXX 600 at 720 by the end of 2027.

## Why it matters now

These calls land in a rough week. Gold is heading for its worst week in eight months and bitcoin hit its lowest since July, as we reported in our stories on [gold's slide](/articles/2026-11-20-gold-heads-for-worst-week-in-eight-months-down-8-percent-3513-low/) and [bitcoin's low](/articles/2026-11-20-bitcoin-falls-61225-lowest-since-july-ibit-record-outflow/). Fed officials have also warned that the AI boom is adding to inflation.

A year-end target is a forecast, not a promise, and strategists' calls have often missed by wide margins. What the list does show is that the big houses still expect earnings to carry stocks higher, even with rates rising. The next test is how they adjust if the Fed hikes more than SocGen expects.
