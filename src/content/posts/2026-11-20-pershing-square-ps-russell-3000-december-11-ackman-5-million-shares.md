Bill Ackman has a message for whoever has been selling his company's stock. "Pershing Square Inc. $PS is scheduled to be added to the Russell 3000 Index on December 11th, which is estimated to require the purchase of ~5 million shares of stock by passive funds that track the index," he [posted on X](https://x.com/BillAckman/status/2123460497391468962) on Thursday. "I don't [know] who has been selling over the last few days, but I thought they might want to know."

## What FTSE Russell has published

Pershing Square, ticker PS, appears on FTSE Russell's [preliminary list of Russell 3000 additions](https://www.lseg.com/content/dam/ftse-russell/en_us/documents/other/ru3000-additions-20261113.pdf) dated 13 November. According to the index provider's [schedule](https://www.lseg.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/ftse-russell/2026/ftse-russell-announces-december-2026-russell-us-indexes-reconstitution-schedule), the changes take effect after the US market close on 11 December. FTSE Russell says this is the first December reconstitution of its US indexes in more than 30 years, part of its move to rebalancing twice a year.

The 5 million share figure is Ackman's own estimate. FTSE Russell does not publish how many shares index funds will need to buy.

## Why index inclusion moves stocks

Funds that track an index have to own its members in proportion to their weight, whatever the price. When a company joins, those funds must buy it, usually at or near the close on the effective day. Traders often try to get in ahead of that demand, which can lift the price in the weeks before. Ackman's point about "closet index trackers" is that active funds which closely follow a benchmark may buy too.

Index changes can push a stock down as well. When MSCI removed Strategy from its indexes this month, tracking funds had to sell, as covered in our [MSCI story](/articles/2026-11-12-msci-removes-strategy-from-indexes/).

## What to watch

The 13 November list is preliminary, and FTSE Russell can update it before the effective date. Watch trading volume in PS in the days before 11 December, and especially in the closing auction that day.
