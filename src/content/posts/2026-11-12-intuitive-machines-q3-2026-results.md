Intuitive Machines, the Houston space infrastructure company listed on Nasdaq as LUNR, reported its largest quarter so far on Thursday. In its [third-quarter results release](https://www.morningstar.com/news/globe-newswire/9876278/intuitive-machines-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results-reports-record-financials-across-the-board-revenue-profitability-positive-cash-flow-and-backlog), the company said revenue for the three months to 30 September reached $269.3 million, compared with $52.4 million a year earlier. [Reuters reported](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com,2026:newsml_L2N46T0O6:0-intuitive-machines-q3-revenue-beats-estimates-on-satellite-production-business/) that the figure beat analysts' estimates, driven by the satellite production business.

## The numbers

Product revenue, a line the company did not have a year ago, was $215.1 million, and service revenue was $54.1 million. Intuitive Machines put gross margin at $69 million, or 26%, helped in part by what it called net favourable one-time program adjustments. The net loss narrowed to $3.4 million from $10.0 million, a loss of 2 cents per Class A share, and adjusted EBITDA was positive $8.3 million against a loss of $13.2 million a year earlier.

Cash from operations was a positive $66 million in the quarter and capital spending $33 million, which the company said produced $33 million of free cash flow. For the first nine months of the year, free cash flow remained negative at $112.3 million. Cash and equivalents stood at $463.8 million at the end of September.

## Backlog and contracts

Contracted backlog rose to about $1.87 billion from $213 million at the end of 2025, including backlog acquired with the Lanteris and Goonhilly deals. The company said it booked $450 million of new awards and acquired backlog in the quarter and was named a vendor on NASA's Commercial Lunar Payload Services 2 contract in the fourth quarter. Chief executive Steve Altemus said the IM-3 mission and the first Altus-1 lunar satellite remain on track for a planned launch in the March to April timeframe.

## Outlook

Intuitive Machines kept its full-year 2026 guidance of $920 million to $950 million in revenue and positive adjusted EBITDA. Adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow are non-GAAP measures that the company reconciles in its release.
