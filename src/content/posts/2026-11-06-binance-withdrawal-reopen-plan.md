Binance said on 6 November 2026 that it is working to reopen withdrawals for several tokens by the end of next week, and that it also aims to restore withdrawals for tokens on networks that are already back online.

In an [update on X](https://x.com/binance/status/2118657118014496819), the exchange wrote that it is working to reopen withdrawals for HYPE, INJ, APT, PLASMA, NEAR, and LTC by the end of next week. The same post said Binance also aims to reopen withdrawals for all tokens on networks that are already restored, including BSC, ETH, MATIC, ARB, POL, OP, and others, by then. The company thanked users for their patience. That is the full substance of the public update; Binance did not add new incident details or compensation terms in that post.

## What this is, and what it is not

The message is an operations timeline from the exchange after a period when some withdrawals were blocked. It names target tokens and restored networks, and it sets an end of next week goal. It does not, on its face, confirm that every listed asset will clear that deadline, or explain the root cause beyond the implication that network and withdrawal systems are being restored in stages.

Users waiting on those assets should treat the post as Binance's current plan, and check the exchange's own status channels for any slip. Secondary reporting may fill in more context on the outage; this piece sticks to the company's stated reopen targets.

This is crypto market news about an exchange operations update. It is not investment advice, not a recommendation to deposit, withdraw, trade, or hold any token, and not a guarantee that Binance will meet the stated timeline.
