Anthropic began rolling out a Personal Finance feature in Claude on 5 November 2026, letting US subscribers on the Pro, Max and Ultra plans connect selected bank and card accounts and ask money questions in everyday language. Claude [announced the beta on X](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2118403114890137895) the same day, and Anthropic’s [product page](https://claude.com/resources/articles/introducing-personal-finance-in-claude) says access is read-only.

Claude’s X post put the limits plainly:

> “Access is read-only, and Claude can't move, manage, or invest your money.”

## How the connection works

[Plaid](https://plaid.com/blog/plaid-anthropic-financial-insights-in-claude/) said on 5 November 2026 that it is powering the account links. Users authenticate through Plaid themselves, so Claude does not ask for or see bank passwords, according to Anthropic. For accounts a user chooses to connect, Anthropic says Claude can see the bank name, account name, type, last four digits, balances and credit limits, and up to two years of transactions.

Anthropic says Personal Finance has no ads or sponsored product recommendations, and that Claude does not offer its own credit cards, loans or savings accounts. Conversations inside the Personal Finance hub are not used to train models, per Anthropic’s page; privacy settings outside the hub still apply, and users can unlink an account at any time.

## What Anthropic says people can ask

Anthropic’s examples include categorising monthly spending, comparing it with prior months, listing bills due before payday, flagging duplicate charges or unexpected fees, and explaining terms such as APR with interest totals drawn from connected transactions. The company also says Claude can build charts or calendars from numbers the user chooses. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1027652/anthropic-claude-plaid-personal-finance) noted the rollout follows a similar ChatGPT feature that also uses Plaid.

Anthropic stresses that Claude can make mistakes, that important figures should be checked with the user’s bank, and that Personal Finance “isn’t designed to give investment advice.” The company frames the feature as a first step toward working alongside licensed professionals and regulated firms for larger money decisions.

## Caveats for readers

This is product news about a US beta opening this week. It is not investment advice, not a recommendation to connect accounts, and not a judgment on whether the feature is right for any household. Capability claims, data scopes and privacy terms come from Anthropic, Plaid and secondary coverage dated 5 November 2026. Availability can change as the beta rolls out.
