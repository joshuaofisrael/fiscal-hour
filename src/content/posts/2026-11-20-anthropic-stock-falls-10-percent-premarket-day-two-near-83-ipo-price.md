The euphoria lasted about one session. "Anthropic stock, $ANT, extends losses to -10% in premarket trading, now down -25% from its post-IPO high," [The Kobeissi Letter posted on X](https://x.com/KobeissiLetter/status/2123754558279319884) at about 7:25 a.m. ET on Friday. "The stock is now 5% away from its IPO price of $83/share."

## From a 39% spike to a premarket slide

On Thursday Anthropic opened at $111.50, well above the $83 price set the night before, as we covered in our [debut report](/articles/2026-11-19-anthropic-opens-111-50-nasdaq-debut-34-percent-above-ipo-price/). [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/anthropic-stock-ai-amd-nvidia.html) says the shares closed the first session at $97.17, up 17% on the offer price but well off the intraday high. By Friday morning CNBC had the stock down as much as 13% before the bell, as a wider sell-off in AI names hit chipmakers too.

The early tape moved fast. [StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/news/anthropic-falls-premarket-second-trading-day/) logged the stock at $90.97, down 6.4%, just before 6 a.m. ET. A 10% drop from Thursday's close puts the price near $87.50, which matches Kobeissi's "5% away" from $83.

## Why the IPO price matters now

For investors who bought at the offer, $83 is the line between a paper gain and a loss. Banks that run an IPO can support a stock near its offer price in the first weeks, but they rarely say whether they are doing so, and nothing published this morning says they are. The deal itself, priced above its range on 980 million shares, is in our [pricing story](/articles/2026-11-19-anthropic-ipo-priced-83-above-range-980-million-shares/).

Day-two drops are common for hot listings, as early buyers take quick profits. What is different here is the backdrop: CNBC ties Friday's move to a broader slide in AI stocks, with Nvidia and AMD also lower.

## What to watch

Premarket prices are thin and can swing sharply once the regular session opens at 9:30 a.m. ET. The level to watch is $83. A close below it would leave every IPO buyer underwater after only two days of trading.

This article was published before the opening bell, and the premarket figures above may have changed since.
