Bitcoin's slide found a new floor on Friday morning. "Bitcoin falls to 4.5-month low of $61,225," [Walter Bloomberg posted on X](https://x.com/DeItaone/status/2123752584414392720) at 7:17 a.m. ET, citing LSEG data. Minutes earlier the same account [reported](https://x.com/DeItaone/status/2123748975148957995) that the Cboe Volatility Index, Wall Street's fear gauge, had risen above 30.

## Three down days and record ETF selling

[CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/20/bitcoin-drops-for-third-straight-day-as-asian-tech-shares-slide) describes it as bitcoin's third straight daily loss, with the price touching about $61,200 before bouncing back above $61,700. Two weeks earlier the same market was being described at about $76,000 after an earlier wave of liquidations, in the [6 November bitcoin story](/articles/2026-11-06-bitcoin-steadies-near-76000-after-liquidations/). Much of the selling this week has shown up in exchange-traded funds. CoinDesk says BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT) recorded a record $541.1 million outflow on Thursday.

Leverage made it worse. CoinDesk puts liquidations of crypto derivatives positions at $718.6 million over 24 hours, as falling prices forced out traders who had borrowed to bet on a rise.

## Same story as the rest of the market

Bitcoin is moving with tech stocks. CoinDesk links Friday's drop to a slide in Asian technology shares, and the move came as AI names fell in US premarket trade and the VIX jumped. Higher interest-rate expectations add pressure: Federal Reserve officials have spent the week warning about inflation, as we noted in our [FOMC minutes coverage](/articles/2026-11-19-fomc-minutes-october-2026-rate-hike-ai-debt-financial-stability/).

Companies that hold bitcoin on their balance sheets feel this directly. Strategy has kept raising money through preferred shares to buy more, as set out in our [STRC dividend story](/articles/2026-11-16-strategy-strc-stretch-dividend-12-percent-december-2026/).

## What to watch

Daily ETF flow figures are published after the US close, so Friday's will show whether the record selling continued. Crypto trades all weekend, while stock markets don't. Thin weekend trading can make swings bigger in either direction.

Prices in this article are from Friday morning ET and move constantly.
