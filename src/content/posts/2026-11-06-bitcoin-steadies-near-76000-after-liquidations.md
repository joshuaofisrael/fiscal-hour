Bitcoin steadied around $76,000 on Friday after a sharp technology and artificial intelligence related selloff unsettled broader markets, according to [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/11/06/bitcoin-steadies-near-usd76-000-after-ai-led-selloff).

The report says the cryptocurrency had bounced from approximately $75,200 after Thursday’s market decline. That session was accompanied by more than $1 billion in liquidations, adding to pressure across leveraged crypto positions.

## Selloff backdrop and jobs data

The move placed Bitcoin’s short term performance alongside a wider risk market repricing. CoinDesk linked the immediate backdrop to the AI led selloff in technology shares, while also noting that traders were looking ahead to the latest US jobs figures. The employment report was a near term event for markets assessing the economic outlook.

A recovery towards $76,000 does not by itself establish that the earlier decline has ended. Price levels can change quickly, particularly when leveraged positions are being closed and broader markets are volatile. The CoinDesk account describes the market at the time of publication, not a lasting direction for the asset.

## Liquidations

The report also connects the episode to liquidation activity. Liquidations occur when exchanges or trading venues close leveraged positions after collateral requirements are no longer met. Such forced closures can amplify rapid moves in either direction, although the headline total alone does not show the distribution of losses among traders or venues.

CoinDesk’s market coverage is the basis for the price and liquidation references in this article. No forecast or trading recommendation follows from the reported movement.
