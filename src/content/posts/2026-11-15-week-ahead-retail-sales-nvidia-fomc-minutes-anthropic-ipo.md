US markets head into a busy week after the S&P 500's best weekly gain since August. Economic data, the minutes of the Federal Reserve's October meeting and a run of big retail earnings are scheduled, according to [Morningstar's weekly calendar](https://www.morningstar.com/markets/market-wrap-stocks-up-252-led-by-energy-industrials), and Anthropic plans its stock market debut.

## Tuesday 17 November

The Commerce Department publishes October retail sales and September business inventories. Walt Disney, Home Depot and Nvidia report earnings, with Nvidia's results a key test for AI-related shares.

## Wednesday 18 November

October housing starts and building permits are due, along with the minutes of the Federal Open Market Committee's October meeting. Investors will look for clues on how fast officials want to keep raising rates. On Saturday Boston Fed President Susan Collins said a steady, gradual path of increases seemed appropriate, and markets widely expect a quarter-point rise at the 8 to 9 December meeting, [Reuters reported via CNA](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/world/feds-collins-says-gradual-rate-hikes-best-manage-economic-risks-6552151). Target and Lowe's report results.

Anthropic is expected to set its final IPO price on Wednesday, [The Verge reported](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1031894/anthropic-confirms-its-planning-for-its-shares-to-start-publicly-trading-on-november-19th).

## Thursday 19 November

Anthropic's Class A shares are due to begin trading on the Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. The company has set a range of $70 to $78 a share for 825 million shares, which at the top end could value it at up to about $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis, [CNBC reported](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html). Weekly jobless claims are also due, and Alibaba and Walmart report earnings.

Dates and times can change; company and agency calendars have the final schedule.
