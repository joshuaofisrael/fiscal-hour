Several scheduled releases fall in the week starting Monday, 2 November. All times below are from the publishers' own calendars, converted to London time (GMT) where they were given in another zone. Schedules can change, so check the linked pages for any updates.

Monday, 2 November: Apple has scheduled its conference call on fiscal fourth-quarter results for 2:00 p.m. Pacific time, which is 10:00 p.m. in London, according to its [investor relations page](https://investor.apple.com/investor-relations/event-details/2026/FY-26-Fourth-Quarter-Results/default.aspx). The quarter ended in late September.

Wednesday, 4 November: Eurostat's flash estimate of euro area inflation for October is scheduled for that day, according to [Eurostat's September release](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-euro-indicators/w/2-16102026-ap), which put annual inflation in the euro area at 3.8% in September, up from 3.2% in August.

Thursday, 5 November: the [Bank of England](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy/upcoming-mpc-dates) is due to publish its Monetary Policy Committee decision and minutes together with its November Monetary Policy Report. The Bank's site lists Bank Rate at 3.75% ahead of the announcement.

Friday, 6 November: the US Bureau of Labor Statistics plans to release the Employment Situation report for October at 8:30 a.m. Eastern time, 1:30 p.m. in London, according to its [release schedule](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/empsit.htm). The report includes nonfarm payrolls and the unemployment rate.

This is a calendar of scheduled events, not a forecast of their outcomes. It is not investment advice.
