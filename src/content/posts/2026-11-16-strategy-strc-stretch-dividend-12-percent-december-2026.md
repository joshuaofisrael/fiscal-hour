Strategy will keep the dividend on its Stretch preferred stock, ticker STRC, at 12.00% for December. Executive chairman Michael Saylor [posted on X](https://x.com/saylor/status/2122132729508855992) on the evening of Sunday 15 November (US Eastern time): "Stretch Dividend Rate maintained at 12.00% for December 2026."

## What the company's page shows

Strategy's [STRC metrics page](https://www.strategy.com/strc) lists the variable dividend at 12.00%, with an effective yield of 12.01% based on a price of $99.91 at the market close on 13 November. The page puts STRC's notional value at about $7.99 billion.

The page also shows Strategy holding 850,364 bitcoin, which it says is 4.05% of total supply. Those figures are the company's own.

## Background

Strategy's [press archive](https://www.strategy.com/press) shows it approved semi-monthly STRC dividends in June, set out an STRC dividend policy and buyback authorisations at the end of June, and began repurchasing STRC in July. In October it reported buying back $192 million of STRC. We found no separate press release for the December rate when we published.

## Why it matters

A steady rate suggests Strategy sees no need to raise the payout to support the price, which sat just under $100 at Friday's close. A 12% dividend is a large fixed-income-style commitment, and holders should note that the rate can change each month and that the security's value is tied to a company whose main asset is bitcoin.
