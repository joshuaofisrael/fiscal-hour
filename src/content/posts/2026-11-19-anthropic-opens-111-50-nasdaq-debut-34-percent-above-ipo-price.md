Anthropic's first trade came in well above its offer price. "Breaking: Anthropic opens at $111.50, 34% above the IPO price after raising $107 billion," [Bloomberg posted on X](https://x.com/business/status/2123460211289592129) just before midday ET on Thursday. [CNBC posted](https://x.com/CNBC/status/2123458933570371589) the same opening price minutes earlier, and Nasdaq confirmed that ANT is "now trading".

## The numbers

Anthropic priced 980 million shares at $83 on Wednesday, above its $70 to $78 range, as [the pricing story](/articles/2026-11-19-anthropic-ipo-priced-83-above-range-980-million-shares/) reported that morning. The roadshow range itself is in the [12 November piece](/articles/2026-11-12-anthropic-ipo-roadshow-price-range/). An opening trade of $111.50 is $28.50 above $83. Dividing 28.50 by 83 gives 0.343, or 34.3 percent. That division is ours. Bloomberg and CNBC called the same gap 34 percent.

[CNBC's live coverage](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/anthropic-ipo-ant-live-updates.html) calls it the biggest initial public offering on record and puts the money raised in the IPO at $81.3 billion. Bloomberg's post uses $107 billion. The difference is a separate $26 billion private placement, which [Cointelegraph](https://ct.com/markets/anthropic-shares-expected-to-open-above-83-ipo-price) says brings the combined deal to $107.3 billion.

## A slow climb to the open

The opening indication rose through the morning. The [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/live/c933xxm82mr8t) reported Nasdaq data pointing to $105, then about $110, which it said would value the company at about $2 trillion. Bloomberg also posted that shares were indicated to open at a $2 trillion market valuation. Those valuation figures are the outlets' estimates, not a company number, and they depend on the share count used.

## What happened next

An opening trade is one print. The next morning, before a full session had traded, the shares were lower. That move, including The Kobeissi Letter’s premarket figures, is in [the day-two story](/articles/2026-11-20-anthropic-stock-falls-10-percent-premarket-day-two-near-83-ipo-price/). This page does not restate those later prices as if they were Thursday’s open.

CNBC also reports that retail demand for ANT was the largest in history. The underwriters’ option to buy more shares was still open when the debut story was filed. Earlier stages of the deal are on the [Anthropic tag](/tags/anthropic/).
