The University of Michigan’s Index of Consumer Sentiment was 48.0 in the final October 2026 survey, compared with 48.1 in September and 53.6 in October 2025, the [Surveys of Consumers](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/) reported. That is a month-to-month change of 0.2% and a year-to-year change of 10.4%, on the survey’s own table.

The Current Economic Conditions index was 46.7, down from 50.9 in September and from 58.6 a year earlier, a monthly drop of 8.3% and a yearly drop of 20.3%, [the survey said](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/). The Index of Consumer Expectations rose to 48.8 from 46.3 in September, and stood at 50.3 in October 2025, a monthly gain of 5.4% and a yearly decline of 3.0%.

[Michigan News](https://news.umich.edu/as-concerns-over-purchasing-power-dominate-consumer-sentiment-moves-sideways/), publishing the survey on Oct. 23, said a move of 4.8 points is the minimum monthly change required for 95% significance on the sentiment index. The bar is 6 points for the current-conditions index and for expectations. The headline index moved by a tenth of a point.

Joanne Hsu, the surveys’ director, wrote that sentiment “held steady this month, registering a scant one-tenth of an index point below September.” On the [survey site](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/), she said increases among Republicans and Democrats were balanced by declines among independents. Year-ahead business conditions improved about 14% and stand 16% below last October’s reading, she wrote.

The decline in current conditions “was driven entirely by buying conditions for durables, which plunged 18%” because of higher interest rates and worries about whether those purchases are affordable, Hsu wrote on the [survey page](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/). She said sentiment is 10% below a year ago and 15% below February, “just prior to the Iran conflict.”

Year-ahead inflation expectations rose from 4.6% in September to 4.7%, [the survey said](https://www.sca.isr.umich.edu/). Hsu wrote that the reading is above the 3.4% recorded in February before the Iran conflict began, and above every 2024 reading. Longer-run expectations stepped up from 3.4% to 3.5%, above the 2024 range of 2.8% to 3.2%. Both horizons rose for a second straight month, to the highest readings since May, she wrote.

The survey is a rotating panel at the Institute for Social Research, [Michigan News said](https://news.umich.edu/as-concerns-over-purchasing-power-dominate-consumer-sentiment-moves-sideways/). Each household in the coterminous United States has an equal chance of selection, and interviews are conducted through the month on the web. A separate [Michigan News account](https://news.umich.edu/as-concerns-over-purchasing-power-dominate-consumer-sentiment-moves-sideways/) of the same release covers what respondents said about buying a house.
